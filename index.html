Builder & writer. Detroit-made, Washington-DC-based.

I ship software people pay for, then write about what it taught me. By day I'm a senior engineer at a consumer-finance company, building AI systems that move real money and have to be right. Nights and weekends, I run a small portfolio of my own: a test-prep company, a book-club app, community tools, and a growing shelf of open source.

The through-line is the same everywhere: honest numbers, human voice, software that respects the person using it.

Things that earn

Real products with real customers. No waitlists pretending to be companies.

  • Hacked Summaries earning Audio book summaries and recommendations, grown out of Black Men Book Club. The app I built to get more people who look like me thriving through reading. iOS, 4.7★.

  • AppReviewReady new App Store review readiness for first-time developers — readiness checklist, rejection decoder, queue index. Free check; passes from $19.
  • OffSpec new Design QA for screens that don't have a URL. Drop in a Figma frame and a simulator screenshot; get every mismatch — spacing, color, type — scored and written up as an assignable Jira ticket. Free tier; $9 for 25.
  • AASA.link new Uptime monitoring for iOS Universal Links. The worst outages are the ones nothing reports — a firewall or a stale CDN quietly sends every tap to Safari. Free instant check; $9/mo to be alerted before your users notice.
